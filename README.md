In a world where music is a universal language and playlists serve as personalized soundtracks to our lives, there exists a need for a seamless and creative way to visually represent the essence of each playlist. I was inspired by Spotifys yearly 'Spotify Wrapped' feature that gives a summary of a the users yearly listening data has become an increasing trend where many are so proud to take the time to social media. I wanted a way to combine this viral trend concept with emerging ai generation technology. My proof of concept, poised at the intersection of music and art, aims to personalize the way Spotify users engage with their curated playlists. Spotify, automatically assigns the first four photos of the first songs added album covers in a 4x4 collage or the user can apply a photo themselves. With my AI-powered playlist cover generator, users can effortlessly breathe life into their music collections by transforming them into captivating works of visual art.  By automating the process of cover creation, my app offers users a frictionless way to make their playlists more visually engaging, meaningful, and reflective of the music they contain. This would help better in making a user creating a visual association with the user.
